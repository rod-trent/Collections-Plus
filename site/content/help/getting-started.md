# Getting started

Collections Plus saves web pages, images, notes and quotes into **collections**, like Microsoft Edge Collections did before Microsoft retired it. It's free, open source, and keeps your data on your own devices.

There are two parts, and you can use either or both.

## The browser extension

The extension is where most people start. It opens as a side panel in **Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge** (other Chromium browsers may work too), and it's where you'll do most of your saving and organizing.

1. Install it from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/collections-plus/eekpoobgfoollcmobjeeahonpbjjghia). Edge installs Chrome Web Store extensions too.
2. Click the puzzle-piece icon in your browser's toolbar and **pin** Collections Plus, so its icon is always one click away.
3. Click the icon to open the panel, then **+ New** to create your first collection.
4. Browse to any page and click **＋ Add current page**, or press **Ctrl+Shift+S**.

Coming from Edge Collections? The extension can bring your old collections back. See [Coming from Edge Collections](/help/extension#coming-from-edge-collections).

→ [Using the extension](/help/extension)

## The mobile app

The mobile app puts your collections on your phone. It isn't in an app store: you install it straight from your phone's browser, and it updates itself.

- Open [getcollectionsplus.com/app](https://getcollectionsplus.com/app/) on your phone.
- Add it to your home screen, and it opens like any other app.
- On Android, it also appears in your **Share** menu, so you can save from any app.

→ [The mobile app](/help/mobile)

## Keeping them in sync

The extension and the app share one library through **your own Dropbox**. Edit on either one and the change shows up on the other. Edits from both sides are merged, so nothing gets overwritten.

There's no Collections Plus account, and your collections never pass through our servers: they go straight between your devices and your Dropbox.

→ [Sync with Dropbox](/help/sync)

## Your data stays yours

- Your collections live **in your browser**, on each device you use.
- Sync is optional and goes through **your own** cloud storage.
- AI features are optional, use **your own** AI account, and are off until you turn them on.
- There's no tracking and no analytics.

The [privacy policy](/privacy) has every detail.
