# The mobile app

The Collections Plus mobile app runs in your phone's browser and installs onto your home screen, so there's no app store involved. It opens full screen, works offline, and updates itself.

## Install it

**On Android (Chrome):**

1. Open [getcollectionsplus.com/app](https://getcollectionsplus.com/app/) in Chrome.
2. Tap **Install app** when Chrome offers it. If it doesn't, tap **⋮ → Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
3. Open Collections Plus from your home screen.

**On iPhone or iPad (Safari):**

1. Open [getcollectionsplus.com/app](https://getcollectionsplus.com/app/) in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Collections Plus from your home screen.

Your collections are stored in the app on that phone. To see the ones from your computer, connect Dropbox: see [Sync with Dropbox](/help/sync).

## Find your way around

- **Home** lists your collections and folders, in the same order as on your computer. Pinned collections stay at the top, and the **Reading list** sits above them when you have unread pages.
- Tap a collection to open it. Tap a page to open it in your browser.
- The **⋯** at the top right holds the app's settings and extras: sync, Archive, Trash, new folders, AI settings and importing a backup. Inside a collection, **⋯** holds that collection's options.
- Every page, note and quote has its own **⋮** with **Open**, **Edit**, **Mark as read**, **Mark as done**, **Move to…** and **Delete**. Deleting offers **Undo** for a few seconds.
- The **＋** button at the bottom right adds things.
- Your phone's **back** button closes menus and goes back a screen.

## Save things

**From any app (Android):** once the app is installed, **Collections Plus appears in your phone's Share menu.**

- **Share a page** from Chrome, YouTube, Reddit or any app: pick a collection and tap **Save**. If you've set up auto-file rules on your computer, they pick the collection for you.
- **Share text**, such as a message or part of an email, to save it as a **note**.
- **Select a passage on a web page** in Chrome and share it to save it as a **quote**, with a link back to the page. Tapping that link later jumps straight to the passage. Prefer the page itself? Switch to **Link** before saving.

iPhones don't let web apps into the Share menu yet, so on an iPhone, copy the link and use the **＋** button instead.

**With the ＋ button:**

- On the home screen: **Save a link**, **New collection** or **New folder**.
- Inside a collection: add a **Link**, a **Note** or a **Collection** inside this one.

You can type a web address without `https://`; the app adds it.

## Read through a collection

On your computer, **Open all** opens a whole collection as tabs. Phones open one page at a time, so the app has **Read through** instead.

1. Open a collection (or the Reading list) and tap **▶ Read through**.
2. Tap **Open page** to read the first one. Each page you open is marked as read.
3. Come back to the app and tap **Next**, or swipe left. Swipe right, or tap **Previous**, to go back.

## Checklists and custom fields

- Tap **Checklist** at the top of a collection to show checkboxes, with a running count like "3 of 8 done". Ticks sync with your computer, so you can build a list at your desk and tick it off in the shop.
- **Custom fields** such as price or quantity show on each item. To add or change them, tap the item's **⋮ → Edit**.

## Find things

- Tap **🔍** on the home screen to search every collection, including notes, tags and custom fields.
- Tap a **tag** (on the home screen or on a collection) to see just the collections with that tag.
- **Search by meaning** uses AI to find things that match what you mean, not just the words you typed. See [AI search](/help/ai).

## Organize

- **Sort:** tap the sort button at the top of a list ("Your order") to sort by date or A–Z. This only changes the view on your phone.
- **Rearrange:** tap **Reorder**, then drag the **⠿** handles. On the home screen that's your folders and collections; inside a collection, it's the collections and items in it. Tap **Done** when you're finished.
- **Move a collection:** in the collection, **⋯ → Move to…** puts it in a folder (or a new one), inside another collection, or back at the top.
- **Folders:** tap the **⋮** beside a folder to rename it, give it a color, start a new collection inside it, or delete it. Deleting a folder keeps its collections.
- **Pin**, **Rename**, **Archive** or **Move to Trash** a collection from its **⋯** menu.
- **Share links:** **⋯ → Share links** sends every link in a collection to another app, such as Messages or email.

## Archive and Trash

Both are in the home screen's **⋯** menu.

- **Archive** holds collections you've set aside. **Restore** brings one back. Tap **Reorder** to rearrange the Archive, or tap an archived collection to look inside it without restoring it. You can't edit it there, but **Reorder** rearranges its pages and the collections archived with it.
- **Trash** holds deleted collections and folders for **30 days**, with a countdown. **Restore** brings one back; **Delete forever** removes it now; **Empty Trash** clears everything.

## Updates and offline use

The app updates itself: close it completely and open it again to get the newest version. If you're offline, it still opens and shows your collections, and anything you change syncs once you're back online.
